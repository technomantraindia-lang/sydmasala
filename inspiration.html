✦ CULINARY TECHNIQUE

The Art of the Indian Tadka

Because SYD spices are unadulterated and ground below 40°C, their volatile essential oils react instantly with heat. Follow these 4 gold rules to unlock restaurant-grade flavours.

01

The Crackle Zone (175°C)

Heat ghee or mustard oil until shimmering, but not smoking. Drop whole cumin seeds: they must swell and crackle within 4 seconds without turning black.

02

The 3-Second Chilli Rule

Pure red chilli powder has natural capsaicin oils. Always turn off the flame before adding chilli into hot ghee. 3 seconds off-heat locks in ruby red color without coughing or acrid smoke.

03

The Masala Bhunao Bloom

When sauteing onions and tomatoes, add your Magic Blend with 2 tablespoons of warm water. This prevents delicate spice particles from scorching and unlocks deep umami sweetness.

04

The Finishing Aromatics

Never boil Garam Masala for 20 minutes! Add it in the final 2 minutes of cooking. Stir once and immediately cover with a tight lid for 5 minutes to infuse the steam with pure aroma.
