Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our India sourcing, cold-ground small-batch method, ordering, and trade policies.

All SYD Spices are headquartered and processed at Gala No. 12, Spice Park in Gujarat, India — universally celebrated as Asia's premier whole spice hub. We ethically procure whole cumin, fennel, mustard, chillies, and aromatic whole spices directly from accredited agricultural mandis and trusted farmers before cleaning, slow cold-grinding, and packaging under strict hygienic standards.
Conventional industrial spice mills operate at high speeds, generating friction temperatures exceeding 70°C. This thermal spike causes the volatile essential oils — which provide true aroma, antioxidant properties, and nuanced flavour — to vaporize. At SYD, our slow cold-grinding process regulates temperatures so the natural essential terpenes and authentic warmth remain intact in every grain.

Our Red Chilli Powder contains 100% pure sun-dried whole chillies without artificial red dyes (no Sudan dyes or synthetic pigments). Our Magic Blend and Garam Masala are 100% natural whole ground spices with zero anti-caking agents, MSG, or cereal fillers.
